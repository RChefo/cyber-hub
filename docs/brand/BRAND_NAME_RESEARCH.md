# ExploitX: name and domain research

A desk search of public sources on 2026-11-13 (Mission 13), made after the owner chose the name **ExploitX** and the domain **exploitx.net**.

**This is not legal advice and not a trademark clearance.** The name is the owner's decision and was not changed. Nothing was bought, registered or contacted.

## Summary

* **No registered or pending trademark "EXPLOITX" was found:**
  * in the USPTO;
  * in TMview (which covers EUIPO, the UK, WIPO Madrid filings, India and many national offices);
  * in Trademarkia.
* **The name is already in use in cybersecurity.** The most important prior user is a student cybersecurity club and national CTF in India, "ExploitX". It holds exploitx.org, a GitHub organisation and social accounts.
* **exploitx.io sold game software under the name "ExploitX" in 2026,** most likely cheats. The domain expired in September 2026 and can be registered again by anyone, which is a reputational risk.
* **Trademarks on "EXPLOIT" for software or security exist** in Italy, Australia and the United Kingdom. They need an attorney's review before a public launch.
* **exploitx.net** was registered on 2026-11-13 at 11:58 UTC at Namecheap and serves a parking page. The owner should confirm that the registration is theirs. This session did not verify ownership or DNS.

## Similar names in security

* **Exploit-DB** (OffSec), the dominant public exploit archive. ExploitX may be read as related to it.

* Exploit Pack, Exploit Labs, Hexploits, Exploit Alarm.
* Brands with an "X" in security: IBM X-Force, Cortex XDR/XSIAM, XM Cyber. An "X" suffix adds little distinctiveness.
* "EXPLOIT" is arguably **descriptive** for security software. That may limit how strongly the name can be protected.

## Suggested precautions (the owner's decisions; nothing was bought or registered)

1. **Before a public launch, have a trademark attorney clear "ExploitX".** Cover the US, EU, UK, India and Australia in classes 9 and 42 (possibly 41 and 45). Ask in particular about:
   * the Italian EXPLOIT, Australian EXPLOIT SECURITY and UK Exploitr marks;
   * how descriptive "exploit" is;
   * the Chennai club's prior use.
2. **Confirm that exploitx.net is in the owner's Namecheap account,** then turn on auto-renew and the registrar lock.
3. **Consider defensive domains and handles.** exploitx.dev and exploitx.app are unregistered; exploitx.io may drop soon. Consistent handles, for example GitHub `exploitxhq`, would also help. The repository stays `RChefo/cyber-hub` until the owner decides otherwise.
4. **Always pair the name with its descriptor:** "ExploitX — cybersecurity intelligence and security research platform". Keep the visual identity away from gaming and "cheat" styling. The identity in [VISUAL_IDENTITY.md](VISUAL_IDENTITY.md) does this: no neon green, no glitch or hacker imagery.
5. **Before release, check the website's domain and the signed installers** against antivirus engines and URL-category filters. Names with "exploit" can be classified as hacking-related.
6. **Watch for new filings:** a TMview or USPTO alert for EXPLOITX, and a watch on exploitx.io.
